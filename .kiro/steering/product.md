# Product

TutorForMe is a personal tutor for school students working through biology, chemistry, and mathematics (olympiad and exam problems, about 9th grade unless the question is clearly harder).

Slogan: **Learn smarter. Grow faster.**

## Who it is for

A student who can already open the textbook and still cannot see which line they are stuck on. The product names that line, walks it in a few steps, shows a formula when one helps, and ends with one check.

## What it does

TutorForMe is a subject workspace, not a chat box. Biology, chemistry, and mathematics each keep their own desk.

- Desk: the next unfinished station, the rest of the path as a list, then a walk-or-solve tool for a stuck point the path does not cover.
- Path: a fixed sequence. One flagship walkthrough per subject, plus shorter stations in the same lesson shape.
- Practice: the checks from that path, one at a time, with the reason after the choice.
- Notebook: notes the student files, including a step taken from a session.
- A live walk or solve still returns one lesson. It does not replace the path.
- The interface is in English.
- Formulas rendered as LaTeX, not as a screenshot.

## What it refuses

- A finished answer with no path.
- A second tutor product inside one of the apps. Web and API are two faces of one protocol.
- Accounts, class rosters, or a grade book. Those are other products.
- Pretending a model is a textbook. Biology and chemistry copy stays checkable.

## Success

A student can finish a guided lesson, miss the check, read why, and try again — and can point at the step that was unclear.
